Privacy Policy

Transparency on how visitor information, cookies, third-party advertising, and external links are handled on chandanghanghav.vercel.app.

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal portfolio website operated by Chandan Ghanghav at https://chandanghanghav.vercel.app/ ("the Website"). Protecting visitor privacy is important to us. Because this Website is a static portfolio and informational site, it does not offer user accounts, paid checkouts, or server-side databases that store personal profiles.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you choose to contact Chandan Ghanghav directly via email at contactchandan0101@gmail.com or through the email inquiry helper on the Contact page, the Website itself does not store your form input on a server. Your email client sends your name, email address, and message directly to our inbox so we can respond to your inquiry.

Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like most websites hosted on modern cloud infrastructure (Vercel), standard HTTP request metadata—such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and access timestamps—may be processed temporarily by hosting infrastructure for security, DDoS mitigation, and reliable content delivery.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

This Website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this Website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to this Website and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on this Website, which are sent directly to your browser.
  • These third-party services automatically receive your IP address when this occurs and use it to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and/or personalize advertising content.
  • Chandan Ghanghav does not have access to or control over the cookies used by third-party advertisers.

How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses data when you use partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, or opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can disable or clear cookies through your individual browser settings at any time. Please refer to your browser's official help documentation (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) for instructions on managing cookie preferences. Disabling cookies does not prevent you from reading the portfolio or case studies on this Website.

5. External Links & Project Websites

This Website links to external web projects (including CareerZoon, PhotoPro, and GlobalTalk) and third-party platforms (including GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube). All external links open with rel="noopener noreferrer" to protect your browsing context. Once you leave this Website, we are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites; we encourage you to review the privacy policy of each site you visit.

6. Children's Information

This Website is intended for a general audience interested in web development, software projects, and digital content. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information via email, please contact us so we can promptly delete the correspondence.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Data Retention

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the EEA under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal communications you have sent to us via email. Since this Website does not maintain a user database, any personal data shared with us is limited to email correspondence, which you may request to have deleted at any time.

8. Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements, advertising partners, or site features. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
